Download

        
Saves a dated .pobak of all on-chip settings: network, Bluetooth, mouse, keyboard, SD folders, UI, display, Wi‑Fi profiles (passwords included), plus NVS prefs (Expose on boot, boot tool, USB serial, device log).

Factory reset

        
Wipes selected on-chip data and reloads firmware defaults. Does not format the microSD card. SoftAP may return to PocketOps / pocketops.

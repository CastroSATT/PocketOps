Audio/
======

What belongs here
-----------------
Sound files and small audio utilities for the field kit.

Typical contents
----------------
  - Short voice notes / site recordings (m4a, wav, mp3)
  - Test tones (speaker / headphone checks)
  - Portable audio players or converters (optional)
  - Hold-music or demo clips if you support AV desks

Tips
----
  Keep clips short; audio fills a card quickly.
  Prefer widely playable formats (mp3 / wav) on unknown PCs.

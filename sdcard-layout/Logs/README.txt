Logs/
=====

What belongs here
-----------------
Stuff you *write* while working — not installers.

Typical contents
----------------
  - Session notes (txt / md)
  - ipconfig / ifconfig / route dumps
  - SMART / HWiNFO exports
  - Serial / UART capture logs from PocketOps or other adapters
  - Photos of error screens (jpg/png)
  - Customer site checklist filled in on-site

Tips
----
  Use dated filenames: 2026-10-19_site-router.txt
  Clear old logs when the card fills up; this folder grows fast.

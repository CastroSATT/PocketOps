PC/Utilities/
=============

Everyday fix-it tools.

Typical contents
----------------
  - 7-Zip (portable or extra)
  - Notepad++ (portable)
  - Everything (voidtools) portable search
  - Sysinternals Suite (subset: Process Explorer, Autoruns, TCPView)
  - Rufus (USB image writer)
  - Ventoy (optional — large; keep elsewhere if space is tight)
  - Hash checkers (SHA256, certutil notes)

Tips
----
  7-Zip + Notepad++ solve a surprising amount of desk-side work.
